106 AI Startups to Cold Email for an Internship (India, Remote and Live Openings)

Published September 2026 · avivashishta.com

You commented intern, so here's the full list: 106 AI startups, split into five groups so you know who to email first. I pulled them from Y Combinator's company directory (through the open yc-oss/api dataset, refreshed 27 September 2026), live Summer 2027 postings on SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships, and India listings on PrepNPlaced and Wellfound.

The short version: cold email means you write to them even when there is no job post. Small teams read their own inbox. Build one small thing for the company, put the link in line one, and send 10 good emails instead of 100 copy-paste ones. Openings change daily, so check the link before you send.

How to use this list

  1. If you're in India, start with groups 1 and 2. Group 3 has live postings, but they're mostly in-person in the US, Canada and the UK.
  2. Open the company's YC page. The founders are listed there. Find them on LinkedIn or X, and use the contact on the company site.
  3. Build something small for them first: a demo, a bug fix, an integration. Link it in the first line.
  4. Follow up once after 5 days, then move on.

The email I'd send

Subject: Built a small [thing] for [Company], internship?

Hi [First name],

I'm [Name], a [year] CS student at [college]. I use [their product / read their launch post] and built a small [demo / fix / integration] that [does X]: [link to live demo] ([GitHub link]).

I'd love to intern with the team, remote or in person, for [dates]. I've shipped [1 line: your best project with a number, e.g. "a RAG app 300 students use"].

Happy to do a short paid trial task. Thanks for reading.

[Name]
[Portfolio link]

How I'd use this

  • Pick 10 from groups 1, 2 and 4. Read their site, use the product, find one thing you could build or fix.
  • Build that thing in a weekend. A live link in the first line of your email does more than any resume.
  • Track it in a sheet: company, founder, date sent, reply. Treat it like a sales pipeline, because it is one.
  • Keep posting what you build. Founders also find you through your posts, not just your emails.

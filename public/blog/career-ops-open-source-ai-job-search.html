career-ops: The Open-Source Repo That Runs Your Job Search

Published September 2026 · avivashishta.com

You commented JOBS, so here's the setup. career-ops is an open-source job search system that runs inside the AI coding CLI you already use: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Gemini CLI. It had 72,559 stars on GitHub on 24 September 2026.

Why this matters right now

Only 5.9% of Indian tech job postings are entry or junior level. That's 908 out of 15,429 LinkedIn and Naukri postings between 29 May and 29 July 2026, or about eight senior roles for every entry role. Only 0.4% are remote. (PrepNPlaced India Tech Hiring Report 2026)

With that few openings, sending more applications doesn't help much. Better applications to the right roles do. That's the job this repo does.

The short version: it scores each job against your CV, flags fake listings and drafts a tailored CV and cover letter. It never applies or sends anything for you. You stay in control of every send.

The repo: career-ops-hq/career-ops ~72.5K ★

github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops

MIT licensed, and everything runs on your machine, so your CV doesn't get uploaded to anyone's servers.

What it does

  1. Scores each listing from 1 to 5 against your CV and shows how it weighted each requirement, so you can see why a role scored low.
  2. Flags scams and ghost jobs before you waste an evening on them.
  3. Drafts an ATS-friendly CV and cover letter for each role. It rewords what you have and doesn't invent experience.
  4. Scans 100+ company job boards for new roles.
  5. Tracks your pipeline in a terminal dashboard and generates interview prep in STAR format.

What it won't do: submit applications, send emails, or push you toward roles that score under 4.0 unless you override it.

Setup

From the project README:

  1. Install Node.js and one AI CLI, for example Claude Code or Codex.
  2. Run npx @santifer/career-ops init.
  3. Write your CV as a markdown file.
  4. Optional: install Playwright if you want PDF CVs.

No paid AI plan? The README lists OpenRouter's free tier and local models as options.

Where to point it if you're in India

  • Wellfound: AI engineer jobs in India. 700+ roles, including AI Engineer Intern listings, mostly in Bengaluru.
  • Internshala: AI fresher jobs. Around 140 openings.

How I'd use it

  • Only apply to roles that score 4 or higher. Read every draft before you send it.
  • Five strong applications a week beat fifty copy-paste ones.
  • Link two shipped projects in every CV: a live URL and a GitHub repo with a real README. The tool can reword your experience, but it can't build projects for you.
  • Use the rejection tracking. If the same gap keeps showing up, that's your next project.

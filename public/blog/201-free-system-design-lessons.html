201 Free System Design Lessons to Crack SDE Interviews in 2027

Published September 2026 · avivashishta.com

You commented design, so here are all 201 lessons, free, with a link for each. They come from two GitHub repos, and I checked every link on 27 September 2026 and dropped the dead ones. A "lesson" here is one page, video, solved problem or paper you can finish in one sitting. No paid books or courses.

The short version: 80 lessons from the System Design Primer (topic chapters plus 14 solved problems) and 121 from Awesome System Design Resources (concepts, 43 interview problems, engineering blogs and the classic papers). Follow the 4-week plan, then use the rest as a reference.

The 4-week plan I'd follow

  1. Week 1: foundations: System design primer "Start here", then the Core Concepts and Networking lessons (scalability, CAP, DNS, load balancing).
  2. Week 2: data: Database and Caching lessons: indexing, sharding, replication, cache strategies, CDNs.
  3. Week 3: patterns: Async communication, microservices, trade-offs. Read the Discord and Slack engineering posts.
  4. Week 4: problems: Do the answering framework, then 2 Easy, 2 Medium and 1 Hard problem out loud, with a timer.

How I'd use this

  • Don't read all 201 in order. Follow the 4-week plan, then use the rest as a reference.
  • For every problem, draw it yourself first (Excalidraw is free), then compare with the solution.
  • Say your answer out loud with a 45-minute timer. Interviews test how you talk through trade-offs, not what you memorised.
  • Build one piece for real: a URL shortener with a cache and a rate limiter, deployed with a live link. That's a project and interview prep in one.

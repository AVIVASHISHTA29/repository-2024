1,401 Free CS Courses from MIT, Stanford and IITs to Learn by 2027

Published September 2026 · avivashishta.com

You commented cs, so here are all 1,401 courses, free, with a link for each. These aren't random YouTube videos. They're full university courses with lecture recordings, from MIT, Stanford, Harvard, CMU, Berkeley, the IITs and more, collected in one GitHub repo. I checked every GitHub-hosted link on 29 September 2026 and dropped the dead ones. The rest come from a list its maintainers keep updated, so if a course has moved, tell me and I'll fix it.

The short version: 1,401 courses across 25 subjects, from DSA to quantum computing. 410 of them are machine learning and AI, including 37 on generative AI and LLMs. If you want an AI engineer job, do the 4 below, build one project after each, and use the rest as a reference.

The 4 I'd start with (and what to build after each)

  1. Harvard CS50 if you're new to coding. Build: your first web app, deployed with a live link.
  2. MIT 6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning. Build: an image classifier trained on your own photos.
  3. Stanford CS224N: NLP with Deep Learning. Build: a sentiment analyser for Instagram comments.
  4. Stanford CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch. Build: a tiny language model trained on your WhatsApp chats.

You don't need all 1,401 courses. You need a few of them, finished, with a project for each on your GitHub.

How I'd use this

  • Pick one course and finish it before starting another. Ten half-watched courses teach you less than one finished one.
  • Do the assignments, not just the lectures. Most of these courses publish their problem sets for free.
  • Build one project after every course and push it to GitHub with a README. That's what a recruiter actually looks at.
  • Watch at 1.5x and pause to code along. You'll get through a full semester in a few weekends.
